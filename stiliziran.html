Sonar. Every screen,
mobile and desktop

Second iteration of the interface, rebuilt from the review of v1. The concept document (docs/deliverables/SONAR_KONCEPT.pdf) defines what each screen must do; the review comments override it where they differ. Every screen is shown twice from the same markup: a 390 px phone and a 1280 px desktop (scaled down). Dashed orange outlines mark optional features, which you can hide from the toolbar. All names, numbers and images are invented placeholders.
